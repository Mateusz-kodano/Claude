## Q1. Legal entity, CIF, address, founders/shareholders/administrators, investors

### Takeaway
The brand is operated by **WOODYS BCN, S.L.** (CIF/NIF **B66156464**), Calle Francesc Santcliment 12, 08500 Vic (Barcelona), incorporated 8 Nov 2013. ### Inferences
- **Conflict on FY2024/FY2025.** Modaes (snippet) says Woodys "exceeded EUR 18.5m in sales in 2025, +32% vs the previous year". That is hard to reconcile with the registry (+5.56% in 2024) and the press FY2023 figure (17–17.5m). There are two internally consistent readings:
  - (A) Press figures equal registry revenue: 2022 about 14.0m, 2023 about 17.5m (14 × 1.2549 ≈ 17.57), 2024 about 18.5m (17.57 × 1.0556 ≈ 18.55). The Modaes "18.5m" headline would then be FY2024, published in 2025, and the "+32%" in the snippet would be a summarisation error or refer to another metric, such as Asia growth. The arithmetic match to 18.5m makes this reading plausible.
  - (B) The registry importe neto is lower than the press "sales" figures: roughly 2022 about 10.6m, 2023 about 13.3m, 2024 about 14.0m, and FY2025 about 18.5m (+32%). The national ranking position (about #17,300 in 2024) is loosely consistent with low-to-mid teens EUR m, but this is a weak heuristic, not evidence.
  - **Treat FY2024 as "about EUR 18.5m if reading A; not verified" and FY2025 as "not verified".** A nota simple or depósito de cuentas from the Registro Mercantil (or an einforma/Informa paid report) would settle it.
- All press revenue figures are company statements, not audited accounts. Registry growth percentages are derived from the filed individual accounts of WOODYS BCN SL.
- **FY2025 accounts:** Spanish SLs approve accounts within 6 months of year-end (by 30 June 2026) and file within 1 month after that (about end of July 2026). On 29 Sep 2026 the FY2025 accounts may be filed at the Registro Mercantil, but no aggregator snippet showed FY2025 registry data. The latest reflected year is 2024.
- The fiscal year is assumed to be the calendar year ending 31 December. Every press figure is reported per calendar year and no other year-end is mentioned. This is not explicitly verified.
- Profitability: EBIT and net result fell about 20–24% in 2023 even though sales rose about 25%. This coincides with the EUR 4m investment in the new 3,000–4,000 m² HQ and robotised warehouse in Vic (higher depreciation and costs) and with international expansion.
- Headcount: registry figures (20–30 employees) likely cover only employees on the company's own payroll. Press figures (100–110) probably include freelance commercial agents (60+). Central staff is about 40.

## Q3. Distribution: countries, export share, subsidiaries abroad

### Takeaway
Woodys is a heavily export-driven wholesaler. About 80–85% of sales are outside Spain, the brand is in 70–75 countries and about 15,000 points of sale, and its core markets are Italy, France, Germany and Spain. It sells through its own agents and third-party distributors, for example Villa Eyewear in the USA since Feb 2024. No foreign subsidiaries were found.

## Q4. Other brands, sub-lines, and other companies of the founders

### Takeaway
The owner runs essentially a **single-brand company**: Woodys (Woodys Barcelona, rebranded as Woodys Eyewear around 2021), with optical and sun collections and a premium sub-line, **Woodys Atelier**. No kids line, second label or third-party brand distribution was found. The founder's family has separate companies: brother **Miquel Dosta Genís** runs **Reflect Eyewear SL / "Visionario"**, a Barcelona sunglasses retail brand, and the family has **Murzuk SL**, a pelts trade and real-estate company that absorbed FDGUNIT SL.

### Cited Findings
- Rebrand: "Woodys Barcelona" became "Woodys Eyewear", with a new logo and a new domain, woodyseyewear.com, to be a more global brand. It was announced alongside the new 3,000 m² logistics centre due mid-2022. — [Modaengafas](https://modaengafas.com/woodys-barcelona-se-trasforma-en-woodys-eyewear-para-convertirse-en-marca-global) (snippet)
- **Woodys Atelier** is a premium sub-line of handmade frames in premium acetate with special hinges, sold as limited editions at about EUR 230–250. It launched or was relaunched around 2024. — [FashionNetwork 2024](https://es.fashionnetwork.com/news/Woodys-eyewear-apunta-a-los-20-millones-de-euros-gracias-a-su-despliegue-internacional,1660395.html); [Bermeo Óptika](https://bermeooptika.com/atelier-de-woodys/); [Modaengafas tag page](https://modaengafas.com/etiqueta/woodys) (snippets)
