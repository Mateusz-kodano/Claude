## 1. Legal entities, owner, directors, PSC, ownership changes

### Takeaway
William Morris London is no longer independent. On **21 Sep 2022** its shareholders (led by founder Robert William Morris) sold the business to the Danish **Design Eyewear Group (DEG)** for an undisclosed price. The two original UK operating companies were then struck off (Nov 2023 and Jan 2024). The brand is now run in the UK by DEG's subsidiary **Design Eyewear Group Limited** (company no. 05846419, formerly Pro Design Int. Eyewear UK Ltd), at the former William Morris premises in Hatfield Heath, Essex. The ultimate parent is DEG in Aarhus, Denmark. In **April 2024** DEG was sold by the private-equity firm Erhvervsinvest to a consortium of Danish private investors.
