### Inferences
- **Group and individual accounts differ.** In 2023 the press group figure (about €89.6M implied) is about €12M above the individual S.L. figure (€77.8M). The likeliest reason is that press figures include the foreign subsidiaries (US/Canada, Hong Kong), whose sales to end customers are consolidated, while the S.L. books intra-group sales at transfer prices. Tressol's "consolidated accounts" auditor mention suggests **consolidated accounts are filed at the TRESSOL SA level**, not at Etnia Eyewear Culture SL. Not verified.
- **Growth has been slow in recent years.** The individual S.L. was flat from 2022 to 2024 (about +0.5% and +1% a year). Group revenue grew about 6–7% a year from 2021 to 2025 if the targets were met. Both are far below earlier plans: €120M by 2023 (2019 plan) and €140M by 2024 (2022 plan). See Q3.
- Net profit and EBIT rose slightly faster than sales in 2023, so margins were stable to slightly better. The absolute margin level is unknown.
- Equity of about €40.7M on about €78M sales means a well-capitalised company, since equity is more than half of annual turnover.

## 3. Growth trend, export share, countries, points of sale, own stores, subsidiaries

### Takeaway
Etnia is a strongly **export-led** wholesale business: about **85% of sales are made outside Spain**. It sells in **50+ countries** through about **15,000 optical points of sale**, mostly independent opticians. It has subsidiaries and offices in **Miami, Vancouver and Hong Kong**, and a presence in Shanghai was reported in 2021. It owns **100% of its US and Canada distribution**. It runs a few own stores, led by the **Born flagship in Barcelona** and **Paseo de Gràcia 44**. Plans for 6–8 flagships worldwide have mostly not been carried out.

## 4. Other brands and other companies of the owner

### Takeaway
The owner runs a **multi-brand eyewear group, "Etnia Eyewear Culture"**, created in January 2022. According to the September 2026 announcements it consists of **Etnia Barcelona** (about 85% of sales), **lool** (bought in 2021), **The Readers** (ready-made readers), **Pellicer** (premium line) and **Moi Aussi** (an art-led brand). **Allpoets** (a sustainable bio-acetate brand launched in 2022) is **not listed** in 2026, and its status is unclear. Outside eyewear, the Pellicer family, through **Tressol SA**, owns **Villas Etnia** (a holiday-villa complex in Menorca) and prime Barcelona property.
