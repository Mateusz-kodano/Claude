## 1. Who is the licensee/manufacturer of Ana Hickmann eyewear (legal name, CNPJ, location, owners)? Is it part of a larger group? Has the license changed hands?

### Takeaway
GO Eyewear makes and sells Ana Hickmann eyewear. It is a Brazilian, privately held company, formerly called "General Optical" / "GO". Its legal entity is **KENERSON INDÚSTRIA E COMÉRCIO DE PRODUTOS ÓPTICOS LTDA**, CNPJ **07.019.231/0001-96** (head office in São Paulo, opened 19/07/2004). Its partners are **GO Participações Ltda** and **Ji Xiaoci (Joyci Lin)**, who has been CEO since 2009. The brand was co-created by Ana Hickmann and GO in 2002 and has stayed with GO ever since. As of April 2026 there is no sign of any change of licensee. In Europe the brand is distributed by the group's Portuguese company **GO Eyewear, S.A.** (NIF 509072577, Odivelas/Lisbon).

### Inferences
- The Ana Hickmann eyewear licensee has been the same company, GO Eyewear / General Optical, from 2002–2003 to at least April 2026. **I found no evidence that the license changed hands.**
- It is not clear whether the Ana Hickmann *eyewear* trademark belongs to GO or to Ana Hickmann's company. Two things point in different directions:
  - The old "General Optical is the owner of the brands… Ana Hickmann" wording suggests GO owns or controls the eyewear brand.
  - Press on Hickmann Serviços (section 3) says Hserv holds the licensing contracts for "óculos". That fits a license/royalty contract with GO.
  - The most likely set-up is a **royalty-bearing license from Hickmann Serviços to GO**. This is **not verified**.
- Kenerson/GO does not appear to belong to a foreign group. GO says it is 100% Brazilian capital, and its partners are a holding company (GO Participações) and the CEO. The European arm (GO Eyewear S.A., Portugal) looks like a group subsidiary or sister company. **Who owns the Portuguese S.A. was not verified.**

## 3. Ana Hickmann's own brand-management companies (Hickmann Serviços etc.): finances, legal situation, and any effect on the eyewear license

### Takeaway
**Hickmann Serviços Ltda ("Hserv")** is the company that holds the licensing contracts for Ana Hickmann-branded products (eyewear, nail polish, beauty articles, image rights). In December 2023 her ex-husband **Alexandre Correa** filed for judicial recovery (recuperação judicial), claiming debts of about **R$ 40 million**. On 18/12/2023 the court held that a partner has no standing to file on the company's behalf. From 2024 to 2026 there has been a long series of disputes with banks and creditors, several of which went Ana Hickmann's way after courts found her signature had been forged. In September 2026 the São Paulo court (TJSP) pointed to R$ 9.8M in liquid funds at the company, a figure she disputes. **I found no evidence that the eyewear license with GO was suspended or affected.** She was still presenting her GO collection in April 2026.

### Cited Findings

## 4. Other brands produced or distributed by the eyewear licensee (GO Eyewear)

### Takeaway
GO Eyewear runs a portfolio of about **10–12 own and licensed Brazilian brands** plus some international distribution. They include Ana Hickmann Eyewear, Hickmann Eyewear, Atitude, Bulget (Occhiali), Evoke, Speedo (licensed), T-Charge, Jolie, Jean Marcel(l), Ferrati and Anima (with Costanza Pascolato, 2022). It also distributes **Mykita** in Brazil. From mid-2017 to about early 2024 it distributed **Kering Eyewear** brands in Brazil (Gucci, Saint Laurent, Bottega Veneta, Cartier, Montblanc, Puma, Alexander McQueen/McQ, Stella McCartney, Boucheron). **In early 2024 that distribution passed to Protus Group (Ascensus Group).** It has also run a hearing-aid business (PHD / Starkey).
