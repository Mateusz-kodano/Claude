## Q1. Legal entity, CIF, address, owners, administrators, investors, restructuring (Gigi Barcelona → Gigi Studios → Offview)

### Takeaway
Gigi Studios eyewear is now made and sold by **OFFVIEW, S.L.** (Sociedad Limitada Unipersonal, CIF **B72903537**, share capital €520,000). The registered address is C/ Bosc Tancat 8, Nave 4, Pol. Ind. Polizur, 08290 Cerdanyola del Vallès (Barcelona).
- **Creation (28 Nov 2022):** the company was set up as a new "GIGI STUDIOS, S.L.". It was one of two companies created in a total demerger (escisión total) of the historic family company. That company was LUIS RAMO, S.L., later renamed GIGI STUDIOS, S.L. (CIF B58388612), and it was struck off on 15 Feb 2023.
- **Rename (16 Jul 2024):** the new company was renamed OFFVIEW, S.L.
- **Ownership now:** Patricia Ramo Falcó (third generation) bought the company from her father, Luis (Lluís) Ramo Pomada, at the end of 2024. She has been sole administrator (administradora única) since 4 Dec 2024.
- **Investors:** I found no private equity, fund or outside investor.

### Inferences
- **Most likely corporate chain:**
  1. LUIS RAMO, S.L. (B58388612, a 1980s-era Barcelona CIF) was renamed GIGI STUDIOS, S.L.
  2. In 2022 it was split in a total demerger into two companies incorporated on 28 Nov 2022. The operating eyewear business went to a new GIGI STUDIOS, S.L. (B72903537, capital €520k). The property went to RAMO PICANYOL, S.L. (B72902398, owned 100% by Luis Ramo).
  3. The old company was struck off on 15 Feb 2023.
  4. The new company was renamed OFFVIEW, S.L. on 16 Jul 2024.

  Empresia and Datoscif show CIF B58388612 for OFFVIEW. That is very likely an aggregator error that merges the two companies. Empresite, Iberinform and Infoempresa all give B72903537, and the CIF series matches a 2022 incorporation.
- **Who owned it before and after:** OFFVIEW is a "sociedad unipersonal", so it has one shareholder. Patricia "bought the company from her father", so the sole shareholder was probably Luis Ramo (directly or through a vehicle) until late 2024 and has been Patricia Ramo Falcó (directly or through a vehicle) since December 2024. This is inferred; I did not see a shareholder filing.
- **Stability risk:** the 2024 family dispute (CEO exit, then return and buy-out) is a governance and continuity point worth raising in any supplier due diligence. Since Dec 2024, ownership and management appear to be in one person, which simplifies decision-making. Two things are unknown: how the buy-out was financed (debt, vendor loan) and whether that debt now sits in the operating company.

- I could not find the current status of Lusther S.A.

Publicly reported revenue rose from about €10.5M (2019) to €17.5M (2021) and **€20.12M (FY2023)**. FY2023 operating result was €2.13M and net profit €1.32M. These are **individual (non-consolidated)** accounts of the operating company, in EUR, and the fiscal year appears to be the calendar year (my inference).
- **FY2022** is muddied by the demerger. Registry-based press puts it at about €17.5M, while the company claimed +30% growth.
- **FY2024:** I found no figures, only ranges (sales €15–30M, 68 employees in 2024).
- **FY2025:** I found no accounts. The company only said in July 2025 that it would "close 2025 with double-digit growth".
- **EBITDA:** not found for any year.
