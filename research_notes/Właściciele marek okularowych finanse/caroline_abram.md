## 1. Legal entity, SIREN, address, managers, shareholders, ownership changes

### Takeaway
The brand is owned and distributed by **OPTA FILAO SARL** (commercial name FILAO PARIS; SIREN 442 182 077; 241 rue de Romainville, 93230 Romainville). This is a small, independent, family-controlled company run by the Hoffman/Abram family. Its current gérante is Annette Hoffman (born 1947), Caroline Abram's mother and founder of the Montmartre optical retailer Opta. I found no evidence that a larger eyewear group or an investor has acquired the company or licenses the brand. It is a family business with sister companies: ESAC SAS (the Paris brand boutique) and GLG EYEWEAR SAS (Caroline's husband's company, at the same address).

- **Caroline Abram** (women, since 2008)
- **Blush by Caroline Abram** (a more affordable, colourful women's line)
- **Tête à Lunettes** (children aged 4–12, since 2014)
